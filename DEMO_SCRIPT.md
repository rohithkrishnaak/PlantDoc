### Timing Breakdown (5 Minutes Total)

| Timestamp | Section | Key Visual / Action | Spoken Script Highlight |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00 – 0:45** | **1. The Problem: Lab vs. Field** | Show slide 2 of Pitch Deck / PlantVillage vs PlantDoc contrast | "Crop diseases destroy up to 40% of Indian farmers' harvest. While lab-trained models boast 99% accuracy, they fail drastically on real smartphone photos taken under direct sunlight, motion blur, and complex soil backgrounds. Worse yet, conventional classifiers emit confident false diagnoses, leading farmers to apply the wrong pesticides." |
| **0:45 – 1:30** | **2. Our Architecture: Quality Gate + Trust Engine** | Show pipeline diagram / `app.py` UI | "To solve this, PlantDoc introduces a two-tier safety architecture. Stage 1 is an OpenCV Image Quality Gate that checks blur, lighting and whether the leaf fills the frame. Stage 2 is a hybrid of two frozen pretrained networks, ConvNeXt and DINOv2, which also rejects photos that don't look like a supported leaf. Stage 3 is a Mamdani Fuzzy Trust Engine. If the photo is poor, it gives a specific retake tip; if the model is unsure, it says 'Not sure, please consult an agricultural expert' instead of guessing." |
| **1:30 – 2:45** | **3. Live Demo: Quality Rejections & Multilingual UI** | Open `http://localhost:8501`, switch language to Hindi / Tamil | "Watch how PlantDoc responds to real-world edge cases. First, we upload an intentionally blurry leaf photo. The Quality Gate detects low Laplacian variance and rejects the photo with the specific tip: 'Image is blurry: hold phone steady and tap to focus.' We switch to Hindi (हिन्दी) or Tamil (தமிழ்), and notice all retake tips, UI elements, and disease names adapt seamlessly." |
| **2:45 – 3:45** | **4. Live Demo: Trusted Diagnosis, Grad-CAM & Care Notes** | Upload clear diseased tomato leaf (Early Blight) | "Now we upload a sharp field photo of a diseased tomato leaf. *(Read the real values from the screen.)* It passes the quality gate with a score of [X], the model's top prediction is [disease] at [Y]% confidence, and the trust engine turns that into a trust score of [Z], above our 0.61 threshold, so it is Trusted. Notice two crucial features: First, our Grad-CAM explainability heatmap visually proves the model focused on the concentric fungal lesions, not the soil. Second, our ICAR/TNAU Agronomic Advisory gives the farmer immediate cultural control methods and safe fungicide dosages." |
| **3:45 – 4:30** | **5. Evaluation & Coverage Curve** | Show `coverage_curve.png` & confusion matrix | "We evaluated PlantDoc on the held-out test split of the official IIT PlantDoc dataset. We removed 7 test photos that also appeared in training. On the remaining 45, the model is right 66.7% of the time when it answers every photo. With the trust engine it answers 42% of photos and is right on 89.5% of those. The coverage curve shows the trade-off: fewer answers, far fewer confident wrong ones." |
| **4:30 – 5:00** | **6. Conclusion & Deployment** | Show GitHub repository and live deployment URL | "PlantDoc is lightweight, mobile-first, and fully deployed. By prioritizing safety, explainability, and linguistic accessibility, we empower Indian farmers with an honest AI crop doctor they can genuinely trust. Thank you!" |
