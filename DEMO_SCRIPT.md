### Timing Breakdown (5 Minutes Total)

| Timestamp | Section | Key Visual / Action | Spoken Script Highlight |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00 – 0:45** | **1. The Problem: Lab vs. Field** | Show slide 2 of Pitch Deck / PlantVillage vs PlantDoc contrast | "Crop diseases destroy up to 40% of Indian farmers' harvest. While lab-trained models boast 99% accuracy, they fail drastically on real smartphone photos taken under direct sunlight, motion blur, and complex soil backgrounds. Worse yet, conventional classifiers emit confident false diagnoses, leading farmers to apply the wrong pesticides." |
| **0:45 – 1:30** | **2. Our Architecture: Quality Gate + Trust Engine** | Show pipeline diagram / `app.py` UI | "To solve this, PlantDoc introduces a two-tier safety architecture. Stage 1 is an OpenCV Image Quality Gate analyzing Laplacian blur, HSV illumination, and leaf canopy coverage. Stage 2 is a fine-tuned EfficientNet-B0 backbone coupled to a Mamdani Fuzzy Trust Engine. If an image is poor or the model is uncertain, PlantDoc never hallucinates—it says 'Not sure, please consult an agricultural expert' with actionable retake tips." |
| **1:30 – 2:45** | **3. Live Demo: Quality Rejections & Multilingual UI** | Open `http://localhost:8501`, switch language to Hindi / Tamil | "Watch how PlantDoc responds to real-world edge cases. First, we upload an intentionally blurry leaf photo. The Quality Gate detects low Laplacian variance and rejects the photo with the specific tip: 'Image is blurry: hold phone steady and tap to focus.' We switch to Hindi (हिन्दी) or Tamil (தமிழ்), and notice all retake tips, UI elements, and disease names adapt seamlessly." |
| **2:45 – 3:45** | **4. Live Demo: Trusted Diagnosis, Grad-CAM & Care Notes** | Upload clear diseased tomato leaf (Early Blight) | "Now we upload a sharp field photo of a diseased tomato leaf. The image passes quality with a 0.72 score. EfficientNet-B0 predicts Tomato Early Blight with 89% confidence. The Fuzzy Trust Engine defuzzifies this into a 0.81 Trust Score (Trusted). Notice two crucial features: First, our Grad-CAM explainability heatmap visually proves the model focused on the concentric fungal lesions, not the soil. Second, our ICAR/TNAU Agronomic Advisory gives the farmer immediate cultural control methods and safe fungicide dosages." |
| **3:45 – 4:30** | **5. Evaluation & Coverage Curve** | Show `coverage_curve.png` & confusion matrix | "We evaluated PlantDoc on the held-out test split of the official IIT PlantDoc dataset. Under our Fuzzy Trust threshold, diagnostic accuracy on accepted field photos rises from 64% unfiltered to over 85% selective precision. The coverage curve proves that our Trust Engine successfully filters out overconfident misdiagnoses." |
| **4:30 – 5:00** | **6. Conclusion & Deployment** | Show GitHub repository and live deployment URL | "PlantDoc is lightweight, mobile-first, and fully deployed. By prioritizing safety, explainability, and linguistic accessibility, we empower Indian farmers with an honest AI crop doctor they can genuinely trust. Thank you!" |
